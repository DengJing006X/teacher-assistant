新教师入职指南

1. 入职流程
新老师需要在入职第一天到人事部报到，领取工牌和教师手册。
然后到教研组 meeting room 参加新教师培训。

2. 课时安排
每位老师每周基本课时为 16 节，超过部分按 1.5 倍计算课时费。
早晚自习课时按 1.2 倍计算。

3. 薪资发放
每月 15 日发放上月工资。
新老师试用期 3 个月，试用期工资为正式工资的 80%。

4. 请假制度
事假需要提前 2 天在系统提交申请，由教研组长审批。
病假需要提供医院证明。

5. 教学资源
教案模板在共享文件夹中，文件名为 "教案模板_v2024.doc"。
课件制作可以使用学校提供的 PPT 模板。

New Teacher Onboarding Guide

1. Onboarding Process
New teachers should report to HR on the first day to collect their badge and teacher handbook.
Then attend new teacher training at the teaching research group meeting room.

2. Class Schedule
Each teacher's basic weekly class hours are 16 periods. Overtime is paid at 1.5x rate.
Morning and evening self-study sessions are paid at 1.2x rate.

3. Salary
Salary is paid on the 15th of each month for the previous month.
Probation period is 3 months with 80% of regular salary.

4. Leave Policy
Personal leave must be submitted 2 days in advance via the system for team leader approval.
Sick leave requires a hospital certificate.

5. Teaching Resources
Lesson plan templates can be found in the shared folder named "lesson_plan_template_v2024.doc".
Use the school's PPT template for courseware creation.
